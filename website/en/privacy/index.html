Bonvrij privacy statement

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Bonvrij is an app for keeping purchases, receipts, invoices and warranty dates. This statement explains what happens to your data. In short: your purchases and proofs of purchase stay on your own phone. We receive nothing. On Android, Google's text recognition does send technical usage data to Google; see "Text recognition" below.

The Dutch version of this statement is at bonvrij.nl/privacy.

Who we are

Bonvrij is an app by Shakeel Bechan. Contact: bonvrij.app@gmail.com

What data the app stores

Only what you enter or add yourself:

  • product details: name, brand, category, serial number or IMEI, store, purchase date, price
  • warranty end dates and reminder dates that you enter yourself
  • proofs of purchase: photos of receipts and PDF or image files
  • preferences, such as your default reminder

This data is stored only on your phone, in a protected area that only Bonvrij can read.

What the app does not do

  • You do not need an account.
  • The app never sends your purchases or proofs of purchase to us or to anyone else. We have no server.
  • No ads and no trackers. We do not collect usage statistics ourselves.
  • No access to your email. You share an invoice with the app yourself if you want to.

Text recognition

Bonvrij reads the text of a receipt or invoice on your phone, using the built-in text recognition of Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android). The recognised text is only used to fill in the form for you and is then discarded. Only what you confirm with "Save" is stored.

iPhone: Apple's text recognition (Vision) runs entirely on your phone and sends nothing.

Android: Bonvrij uses Google ML Kit. Google Play services downloads the recognition model the first time. After that, the text is read on your phone. ML Kit does send technical usage data to Google, which according to Google is used for diagnostics and usage statistics:

  • device information: make, model and Android version
  • the name and version of the app
  • an identifier per installation (according to Google not intended to identify you or your device)
  • performance, settings and error codes of the recognition

The text, photos and PDFs of your receipts are not sent. Google encrypts this data in transit (HTTPS) and, by its own account, does not share it with third parties. We do not receive or see this data, and Bonvrij cannot turn this off. More information: data collected by ML Kit and Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take a photo of a receipt. Only when you choose to.
  • Photos: only the photo you choose yourself.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set yourself. On the Android lock screen the product name is hidden.

The app does not use the microphone.

Backups of your phone

Your phone can include Bonvrij's data in the operating system's own backup: iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android. These backups are encrypted and managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by us. That way your data comes back when you set up a new phone. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Export and deletion

  • Export: in Settings → Export data you create a ZIP file with all your data yourself. You choose where to keep it. The file contains your purchase details, so keep it safe.
  • Deletion: in Settings → Delete all local data you erase everything at once. Deleting the app also erases all its data from your phone. You manage any export or phone backup you made earlier yourself.

No legal advice

Bonvrij does not calculate warranty periods and does not give legal advice. Dates you enter are reminders for yourself.

Changes

If this statement changes, we update the date at the top. If the app ever starts processing data outside your phone (for example an optional cloud backup), we will ask for your consent first and update this statement.
